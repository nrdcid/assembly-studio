    A small assembly workbench
    
        
      
    
      
    
        Place up to eight rotated-planar patches, then define their preparation,
        stabiliser rounds, and logical measurement in order.
      
    
      
    
        Operations execute sequentially. Patches must be
        prepared before use. Logical measurement ends a patch's lifetime. The
        pilot supports X and Z bases and up to 64 total stabiliser rounds.
      
    
      
    
        The surface-code view shows the patch receiving stabiliser measurements
        at the selected round. The spacetime view shows the complete assembly.
        Both use the real Deltakit compiler and renderer.
      
    
      
    
        Projects are saved in this browser and can be downloaded as JSON. Code
        exports contain the editable assembly, before visualization passes.
        Geometry checks and successful IR verification do not certify logical
        correctness or fault tolerance.
